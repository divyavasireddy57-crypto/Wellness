My Journey to Integrative Nutrition

Hi, I’m Kara Lee! As a Registered Dietitian Nutritionist, my mission is to break away from traditional restrictive dieting protocols and help clients build vibrant, sustainable health.

After years of seeing patients cycle through one-size-fits-all meal plans without seeing lasting relief, I shifted my practice toward Functional & Integrative Nutrition. I specialize in gut restoration, hormone harmony, and tissue mineral balancing.

Master of Science in Clinical Nutrition

Specialized research in bio-individual metabolic health and gut microbiome interactions.

Certified Hair Tissue Mineral Analysis (HTMA) Practitioner
